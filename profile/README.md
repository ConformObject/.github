## 🧠 Overview

**Conform Object** is a specialized Blender add-on designed to help users align and adapt objects precisely to the surface of other geometry. It streamlines the process of fitting meshes onto complex shapes, making it especially useful for hard-surface detailing, asset dressing, and environment design.

The tool focuses on automatic and manual surface conformity, allowing objects to follow the curvature and topology of target meshes. This eliminates the need for time-consuming manual adjustments and ensures consistent placement across different surfaces.

Conform Object is particularly valuable when working with decals, panels, accessories, or modular assets that must adhere closely to underlying geometry. It improves accuracy while maintaining flexibility, giving artists control over offset, orientation, and alignment behavior.

The add-on integrates directly into Blender’s workflow and works alongside existing modeling tools. It is designed for both quick placement tasks and more complex scene assembly workflows, making it suitable for game development, visualization, and animation production.
